Overview

Postly is a private writing assistant that helps you draft LinkedIn content. It sends check-in emails, creates draft posts from the information you provide, and can publish a post only after you explicitly approve it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

Plain English: Postly is built to help you write and approve your own posts. It does not scrape LinkedIn, automate activity on LinkedIn’s website, or post without your approval.

Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Your name and email address.
  • Your writing preferences, audience, topics, voice examples, and publishing schedule.
  • Facts, experiences, notes, feedback, and other content you provide through the extension or by replying to Postly emails.
  • Your instructions to approve, edit, skip, or schedule a draft.

LinkedIn connection information

If you choose to connect LinkedIn, Postly receives the OAuth information and access token permitted by LinkedIn for your account. We use this only to identify your connected account and to publish content you have explicitly approved through LinkedIn’s official API.

Technical information

Our service may process basic operational information such as timestamps, delivery status, error logs, and the status of a draft or scheduled post. We do not intentionally collect LinkedIn feed data, contact lists, or browsing activity.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • send you check-in, draft, approval, and service emails;
  • generate and revise drafts based on your own notes and writing preferences;
  • store draft, approval, scheduling, and publishing status;
  • connect and publish through the LinkedIn API when you ask us to do so;
  • maintain security, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot the service.

Postly is not designed to make important decisions about you, profile you for advertising, or sell your personal information.

How information is shared

We share information only as needed to provide the service:

  • AI provider: your submitted notes and relevant writing preferences may be sent to the configured AI provider to generate or revise a draft.
  • Email provider: your email address and message content are processed to send and receive workflow emails.
  • LinkedIn: an explicitly approved post and the authorization needed to publish it are sent through LinkedIn’s official API.
  • Service providers: hosting and database providers may process information on our behalf to operate the service.

We do not sell personal information or use your content for targeted advertising.

LinkedIn integration

LinkedIn access is optional. When you connect your account, you authorize Postly through LinkedIn OAuth. Postly uses the permissions granted by LinkedIn solely to publish content that you have approved. You can disconnect LinkedIn by contacting us; we will remove the stored token associated with your account. Your use of LinkedIn remains subject to LinkedIn’s own terms and privacy policy.

Retention and security

We retain your profile, drafts, approval history, and connected-account information only while your account remains active or as needed to operate the service. We delete or de-identify information when it is no longer needed, subject to legitimate operational or legal requirements.

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including access controls and encryption for stored LinkedIn tokens. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to access, correct, export, or delete your information, disconnect LinkedIn, or stop receiving workflow emails. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights. To make a request, contact us using the details below.

Children

Postly is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the service or applicable requirements change. We will post the revised policy here and update the effective date above.
